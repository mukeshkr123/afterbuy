# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

AfterBuy is for busy online shoppers managing a moderate number of personal
purchases. They open the app for a quick answer: what needs attention, how long
is left, and what action should they take next?

## Product Purpose

AfterBuy keeps receipts, delivery details, return windows, warranties,
reminders, and claims together so a shopper never misses a deadline. Success
means the nearest important deadline is understood at a glance and a new
purchase can be captured without administrative friction.

## Positioning

AfterBuy turns purchase records into timely, actionable protection.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, reassuring. The product should feel quietly premium through
clarity, restraint, and dependable guidance rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic card-heavy SaaS dashboard. Avoid floating-card soup,
decorative gradients, low-contrast gray text, promotional marketplace energy,
or invented controls that fight native platform conventions.

## Design Principles

1. Attention before archive: show what needs action before historical detail.
2. Time must be legible: every deadline communicates date, remaining time, and
   urgency without relying on color alone.
3. Native confidence: preserve familiar iOS and Android navigation and controls.
4. Real data only: never fabricate product images or unsupported metadata.
5. Quiet craft: use disciplined type, spacing, and state feedback instead of
   decorative effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, native text scaling, screen-reader semantics,
reduced-motion behavior, and minimum 44pt iOS / 48dp Android touch targets.
Status and urgency must always have a textual or iconographic cue in addition
to color.
